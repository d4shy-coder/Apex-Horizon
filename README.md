# 🏎️ Apex Horizon

**Apex Horizon** is a fun browser-based driving game where you can race, drive around, and explore.

## 🎮 Features

- 🏎️ Drive around and explore
- 🏁 Racing
- 🚗 Vehicle gameplay
- 🌎 Explore the available environment
- 🎮 Free roam
- ⚡ Fast-paced driving
- 🏆 Have fun and try different ways to play

## 🕹️ Game Modes

### 🌎 Free Roam
Drive around freely and explore the game world at your own pace.

### 🏁 Racing
Take part in races and try to reach the finish line as quickly as possible.
